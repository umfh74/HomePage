Privacy Policy

LinkSaver Mobile & Web Application

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

🔒 Privacy Summary at a Glance

LinkSaver is an offline-first, private bookmarking utility. We do not track you, we do not require account registration, we do not sell your data, and all your saved links, notes, and tags remain stored locally on your own device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how LinkSaver ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your data. LinkSaver is designed to help you organize and bookmark URLs from YouTube, Instagram, articles, GitHub repositories, and the web.

By installing, accessing, or using LinkSaver on Android or Web, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Handle

LinkSaver is built with a privacy-by-design approach. We collect and store only the minimal information required to deliver bookmarking features:

  • User-Provided Content: URLs you input or share into the application, custom titles, user-defined tags (e.g. #video, #dev), and personal notes.
  • Locally Generated Metadata: Derived metadata such as website domain, platform category (YouTube, Instagram, GitHub, Article), and public thumbnail links (e.g. public YouTube video preview images).
  • Read & Favorite Status: Flags indicating whether a bookmark is marked as read or starred as a favorite.

We do NOT collect: Personal identification details (such as names, phone numbers, or passwords), location coordinates, browsing history from other apps, contacts, device identifiers for tracking, or analytics telemetry.

3. Where Your Data is Stored

On-Device Local Storage: All saved links, tags, and notes are saved exclusively inside your device's local database (Android Room SQLite database on Android, or browser local storage on Web).

No bookmark data is uploaded to remote backend servers, cloud databases, or third-party marketing services.

4. Android Permissions & Share Target Intent

The LinkSaver Android application uses the following system capabilities:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Used strictly to fetch public thumbnail previews (such as YouTube video thumbnails) and to open links in your default web browser when tapped.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to check network connectivity before fetching web preview assets.
  • Share Target Intent (Intent.ACTION_SEND): Allows LinkSaver to receive shared URLs directly from third-party apps (such as the YouTube app, Instagram app, or web browsers) when you explicitly choose "LinkSaver" in your device's native share menu.

5. Third-Party Links & Services

When you tap on a saved bookmark, LinkSaver invokes your device's default web browser or appropriate native application (e.g., YouTube app) using standard Android Intents or web hyperlinks.

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party websites or services you visit.

6. Data Retention and Deletion (Your Rights)

Because all your data is stored locally on your device:

  • Item Deletion: You can delete any individual bookmark or tag directly within the application at any time.
  • Complete Data Erasure: You can permanently wipe all saved links and data by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings (Settings → Apps → LinkSaver → Storage → Clear Data) or by uninstalling the application.
  • GDPR & CCPA Compliance: As no personal data is collected or processed on external servers, you maintain complete and sovereign control over your information at all times.

7. Children's Privacy

LinkSaver does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is a general-purpose utility for organizing web links.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements or application updates. Any revisions will be published with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
